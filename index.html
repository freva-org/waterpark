Every dataset on one grid. Read only what you need.

Reanalyses, climate model output, campaign data and satellite products, remapped once onto the same equal-area HEALPix grid and served as multi-resolution Zarr pyramids over S3. Nothing to download and nothing to regrid. Explore at a coarse level, analyse at a fine one, and compare datasets cell by cell.

Compare datasets cell by cell

Section titled “Compare datasets cell by cell”

Two stores on the same grid line up cell for cell, so a model bias against ERA5 is a subtraction. Every dataset is written at a range of levels and frequencies: develop against a coarse monthly store, then change two parts of the URL.

For high levels and small regions, read the tips on chunking and selection first.

What you can count on

Section titled “What you can count on”
No regridding
Everything is HEALPix, nested, on one sphere.
Equal-area cells
A global mean is field.mean(“cell”), with no cos(lat) weights.
Only the bytes you ask for
Zarr chunks over HTTP, fetched as you select.
A pyramid in space and time
Coarse and monthly, or fine and hourly.
No mounted filesystem
The same URL from a laptop, Levante or a batch job.

Each source grid is remapped once, conservatively, with grid-doctor. Coarser levels come from coarsening that result, not from remapping again. The reasoning is in Why HEALPix?, Why Zarr and S3? and Remapping decisions.
